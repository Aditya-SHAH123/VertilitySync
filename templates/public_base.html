VitalitySync
Overview Technology Specifications Safety & Limits

VitalitySync is a medical imaging visualization and research tool. It does not diagnose disease, does not classify findings, and does not provide clinical decisions or treatment recommendations. It is not a certified medical device and makes no claim of clinical validation or regulatory clearance.

Imaging credit. The lung surface shown on this site was reconstructed by this application from a publicly available, de-identified research CT in the LIDC-IDRI collection, distributed by The Cancer Imaging Archive under a Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 licence. It is research data, not a VitalitySync user's study, and no identifiable patient information is present in or derived from it.
