High-fidelity imaging

Built from the imaging data.

Geometry, spacing, and density are carried through from the source study rather than approximated for display.

DICOM geometry

Slice order is derived from the image-orientation normal and patient position — never from filenames. Fallback ordering is flagged when geometry is unavailable.

Physical voxel spacing

Row, column, and slice spacing are read per study and honored during reconstruction, so proportions are not stretched or compressed.

Hounsfield units

Rescale slope and intercept are applied per slice, never assumed constant. When calibration is missing, HU is reported unavailable rather than estimated.

Surface reconstruction

A marching-cubes surface is generated at true physical spacing, with a separate interactive representation for responsiveness and a high-fidelity representation for inspection.

Volumetric rendering

The original voxel intensities can be rendered directly with adjustable window and opacity, for detail a surface alone cannot express.

Synchronized coordinates

A single documented transform maps volume indices to patient-space millimeters, keeping the 3D model and the 2D planes in agreement.

Clipping & measurement

Axial, coronal, and sagittal cut planes move interactively. Distances are computed from physical coordinates, not screen pixels.

Original CT inspection

The source axial, coronal, and sagittal views remain available at all times, with windowing presets and per-voxel HU readout.
